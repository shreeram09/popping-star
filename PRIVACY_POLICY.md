**Privacy Policy for PoppingStar**

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

**1. Overview**
[PoppingStar](https://github.com/shreeram09/popping-star) ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data.

**2. Data Collection and Usage**

* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
* **Local Data Storage:** The app creates and manages local user profiles, preferences (such as theme, sound settings, and orientation), and game progress solely on your device using Android DataStore. This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to any external server or third-party service.

**3. Permissions**
[PoppingStar](https://github.com/shreeram09/popping-star) does not require or request sensitive device permissions (such as camera, contacts, microphone, or location access) to operate.

**4. Third-Party Services & Analytics**
The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, ad networks, tracking technologies, or analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, or AdMob).

**5. Children's Privacy**
Our app is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

**6. Changes to This Privacy Policy**
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page or within the project repository.

**7. Contact Us**
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/shreeram09/popping-star/issues).
